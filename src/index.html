Colophon

Letters that fall into place

This started with a simple question: what does a picture look like if you build it the slow way, one letter at a time?

Each letter is aimed at the part of the image that's furthest off, given the colour that fits best, then thrown in and left to tumble and settle. If it makes the picture better, it stays. If not, it's thrown out. A few thousand letters later, the image shows up.

There's no AI in here, only geometry and arithmetic running in your browser. Your image never leaves the page.

One tip: pick the checkered ground to get letters on a transparent background. A cut-out PNG works especially well.
